# AGENTS.md

Brief for any agent or collaborator working on Brownian. Read `PRODUCT.md` first; it is the source of product truth. This file covers the rules, the code, and how to verify changes.

## Non-negotiable product rules

1. **Information and education only.** No buy/sell recommendations, price targets, allocation instructions, or personalised advice. Do not use "advisor", "advisory", "tips" or "calls" in names or copy (SEBI investment-adviser and research-analyst terms). Frame behaviour as research and questions ("Research links…", "Questions investors use…"), never as instructions.
2. **Every number shows its source.** Figures carry `[S#]` (brief sources) or `[W#]` (web sources) tags that open the source. Where sources disagree, say so (see "Data check"). Unverifiable figures are shown as `n/a`, never estimated or invented.
3. **Interpretation is labelled.** The Wander Index, macro-pressure reading, quadrant and holding impact notes are rules-based readings; they carry "Interpretation, not a forecast" and the page footer disclaimer: "For information only; not investment advice."
4. **Never fabricate** users, testimonials, usage numbers, accuracy or performance claims, press, partnerships or pricing.
5. **Indian conventions.** ₹ crore, Indian digit grouping (`toLocaleString("en-IN")`), times in IST, NSE sectoral indices. US event times are converted to IST.

## Design language

All UI follows `design/DESIGN.md` (imported by `CLAUDE.md`). The app imports `design/brownian.css` from `app/globals.css` and the chart helpers from `design/brownian-charts.js` (it doubles as a CommonJS module), so the design folder stays the single source of tokens and chart code. `app/globals.css` adds layout only and never hard-codes a colour.

## Chart and interaction conventions

- **Readout:** any mark with `data-tip-v` (value, shown bold) and `data-tip-l` (label) gets the shared tooltip (`Brownian.readout()`, installed once by `components/Readout.tsx`) on hover, tap and keyboard focus. Give interactive marks `tabindex="0"`.
- **Charts:** helper charts go through `usePlot` (redraws on container resize); React-drawn SVG charts use `useWidth`. Both sit in `.bw-plot`.
- **Motion:** one entrance per chart, played when it first scrolls into view (`components/charts/rise.ts` adds `.rise`; `usePlot` and `useWidth` call it): lines draw, the Money Moves bands wipe in left to right, the ring lights segment by segment, terrain bars rise back to front (CSS under `.rise`, so skipped under reduced motion). Quiet loops: the masthead plus marks and the `.bw-now` dot. Everything sits inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`; marks are fully drawn at rest. Give any new line `pathLength={1}` and a `--d` delay to have it draw.
- **Indicators to watch (Macro Watch, "The macro backdrop"):** `IndicatorField.tsx` ranks USD/INR, US 10Y, Brent, the dollar index and COMEX gold (D5; the dollar index and gold were added in design-v3 from series already in `data/history/world.csv`, both also in the brief's cross-asset list [S11]) and FPI net equity flows (D4) by week or month against their own five years (`lib/indicatorField.ts`, server-side; client-safe formatting in `lib/fieldValue.ts`); height and colour are the same rank, never an estimate, and a missing week is a gap. Roving tabindex: arrows move along a row, up/down change rows. `ReleaseMetrics.tsx` shows `B.releases` as plain metrics on a stacking `<DataTable>` (latest, forecast, previous with its label, gap vs forecast in pp with ▲/▼, due time or note, impact words; the brief's scheduled prints only, n/a where the sources give none; `IMPACT` lives in `lib/impact.ts`). The full table of 13 indicators stays behind `<More>`. The two added rows are context: they do not change the Macro pressure score, which stays the mean of the seven `B.pressure` levels (the dollar index is already one of them; gold is not), and the page says so. India and US CPI/WPI history is not in the store, so no history is drawn for them.
- **Rails (Macro Watch):** `components/Rail.tsx` (client; draws the line in once, motion allowed only) and CSS `.rail*` are the shared primitive: a real `ol`, line in `--hairline-strong`, "now" in `--dawn`, text on the right on phones. `DayRail.tsx` places the PPI and Fed rows by IST clock time (`lib/clock.ts`; "05:30 Sat" = 29:30) between the brief time and the NSE open and close; spacing follows the clock but is capped and floored. `EventRail.tsx` places `B.events` rows that carry an ISO `date` by days from `B.briefDate` (UTC arithmetic, never `new Date()` for "today"): linear for 7 days, compressed after, with a visible break. Rows without a firm date go under "No exact date" and are never plotted.
- **Theme switch:** `components/ThemeToggle.tsx` is a light/dark `role="switch"`; it starts on the system setting and stores `bw:theme` (`night` | `paper`) on the device.
- **Names:** the flow view is **Money Moves** (renamed from Flow Map in design-v3; the route stays `/flows`, and element ids such as `flow-map` stay so links and anchors do not break); the sentiment index is the **Wander Index** (route `/wander`); the front page is **Markets, Overnight**. Third-party products keep their real names (CNN's Fear & Greed, Tickertape's Market Mood Index).
- **Colour meaning:** price changes are coloured by sign (`<Change>` / `toneOf()`: teal up, ember down, with ▲/▼). Impact on India is written out in words ("Headwind for India"), not implied by colour.
- **Narrow phones (21 Nov 2026):** at 440px and under, the slim header is 80px and its day line takes two lines (date and update time, then open and close), so it never causes sideways scroll.
- **Front-page lede (21 Nov 2026):** `lib/ledePoints.ts` splits `B.lede` into one bullet per sentence without rewording, keeping bold, terms and tags. The small feature tag under it and the footer's "Updated" line were removed.
- **Phone reading (20 Nov 2026):** "Interpretation, not a forecast." shows once per section on the front page's Wander section and once under the hero: `Section interp` and `.ifp` are phone-only, the per-tile copies use `.ifw` (wide-only). Story cards (front-page takeaways, `/developing`) show the headline and why-it-matters line, with the paragraph, figures and tags behind `<More label="Details">`; desktop keeps its order through CSS. The front-page Wander section shows the dial and a plain score line, with readings behind "See the N readings".
- **Phone:** wide tables use `<DataTable stack>` and stack into cards under 620px; charts must fit 400px without sideways scroll. Secondary detail folds behind `<More>` (`components/More.tsx`) under 620px; long `<Diverge>` lists take `phoneRows`. The header is `components/Masthead.tsx`: full on `/` (day line, wordmark, tagline and the Wander Index bars; no descriptor sentence; under 620px the wide 480×200 bars are `display: none` and a `compact` 480×150 strip with larger text sits full-width under the tagline, so the bars are announced once), compact elsewhere, its date and times from the day's record (`lib/market.ts` for session hours).
- **Design rules from the review:** no small uppercase label (eyebrow/kicker) above a heading; put the context line under the heading as `.meta`. No nested cards. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast in both themes.
- **Design language rules:** charts sit in a `.bw-plot` window that is the page's own colour in both themes, with its own Night and Paper data colours); ▲/▼ plus a sign on every change; one `dawn` highlight and one primary button per view; every chart redraws to its container (`Brownian.fit`). See `design/DESIGN.md`.

## Verify every change

0. Portfolio files: `npm run lint:copy` and `npm run test:portfolio`.

1. `npm run history:verify` if you touched the history store or its importers, then `npm run typecheck` and `npm run build`.
2. `npm run check:plots`: every chart panel is the page's colour and no mark or label sticks out of it, in both themes at 1280px and 400px. Then `npm run check`: every view at 1280px and 400px in night and paper; it fails on console errors, failed requests or horizontal page scroll, and saves full-page screenshots to look at. Check the views you touched in the screenshots.
3. Tab through what you changed; the readout, the sheet and the term pop-up must work from the keyboard (Esc closes the sheet or the pop-up and returns focus). `npm run check` also opens the first term on `/` at 400px with Enter and fails if the pop-up is not visible, leaves the viewport, causes sideways scroll, or Esc does not return focus to the term.
4. If you changed `design/`, also open `design/reference/brownian-brief.html`.
