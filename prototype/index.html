            CPI crossed the 6% upper limit

            India retail inflation (CPI) vs RBI target band

            October CPI came in at 6.09%, up from 5.54% in September and above the 5.93% forecast. It is the first breach of the RBI's 6% upper limit since October 2024. Food inflation hit 8.94%.

            Why it matters: with inflation above the band, the RBI is more likely to keep raising rates at its 2–4 Dec meeting. That pressures bond-fund NAVs and rate-sensitive stocks, and can lift new FD rates.

            How to read a results day

            Guidance can beat the quarter

            Cisco rose about 3% after hours on raised full-year guidance. Corteva fell 24.3% on a guidance cut.

            A record isn't always enough

            Applied Materials posted record revenue of $10.41bn with strong guidance and was still flat. The good news was already priced in.

            Margins matter

            HAL grew profit 16% but its margin slipped. Page Industries fell 12.9% after its numbers. Check the margin line, not just profit growth.
